The ``5-text_indentation`` module
=================================

>>> text_indentation = __import__('5-text_indentation').text_indentation

Text ending without a delimiter (no trailing newline is printed):

>>> text_indentation("Hello. How are you? Fine: thanks")  # doctest: +NORMALIZE_WHITESPACE
Hello.
<BLANKLINE>
How are you?
<BLANKLINE>
Fine:
<BLANKLINE>
thanks

Text ending with a delimiter:

>>> text_indentation("Hello.")
Hello.
<BLANKLINE>

Spaces around lines are removed:

>>> text_indentation("  Hi  .   Bye  ")  # doctest: +NORMALIZE_WHITESPACE
Hi  .
<BLANKLINE>
Bye

Text without delimiters:

>>> text_indentation("No delimiter here")  # doctest: +NORMALIZE_WHITESPACE
No delimiter here

Empty text prints nothing:

>>> text_indentation("")
